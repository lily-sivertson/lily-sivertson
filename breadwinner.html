Breadwinner

Breadwinner is a short, 2d pixel art, platformer game in which you play as a piece of bread on a mission to become a sandwich. Explore a kitchen to find ingredients for your sandwich while navigating around obstacles and enemies.
Breadwinner was made in about 3 weeks for a class project in Construct3. I am the sole programmer and designer of this project, and all the art is created by me (with the exception of the font). The sound assets were open source ones from online that I editted. This project was created with a primary focus on art, followed by sound. You can play this game yourself here

timeline: mostly complete! I may go back and change a few things in the future,but for now, this game is considered finished!
