concept art

I create 2d art pieces that explore ideas for objects and environments. I primarily make concept art for objects, but I would like to expand to making more environments and other things. I often use a lot of references and research to guide my process and ensure it aligns with the vision.

Illustration

This is my more detailed artstyle which I have been working on for the longest. I focus a lot on rendering and bright colors here, though my methods for shading differ from piece to piece. This style is always changing and improving as I learn and grow as an artist.

Cartoon art

This is my newer and more simplistic art style that I do many commissions in. I try and emphasize bright colors, playful shapes, and uniform weight line art. I started making art in this style because I wanted to branch out on what I am able to draw without having to do complicated rendering. It was also to practice handpicking shading colors, as I rarely use blending modes for shading in this style.
