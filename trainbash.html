Train Bash

Train Bash! is a 3D, multiplayer party game where you and up to 1-3 friends complete in an intergalactic train demolition derby! Collect various train cars with unique abilities to gain an advantage. Bash your train into your opponents to knock them out of the competition.
Take on the role of a train conductor who was abducted by aliens to compete for their entertainment. Your train has been rebuilt and upgraded with the best alien tech to brave the arena. But are you prepared to do what it takes to win?

timeline: Spring 2027
